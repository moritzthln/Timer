## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-01

First public release.

### Added
- Menu bar countdown with four presets, +5 extend, pause and resume
- Pomodoro cycles with configurable focus, break and long-break lengths
- Optional one-line dedication per session, shown above the countdown
- Focus block in two modes (block marked apps and websites, or allow only
  marked ones) that takes apps out of fullscreen before hiding them
- Emergency mode: a 1–60 minute lockdown to a chosen list, cancelled only by
  a ten-second hold
- Focus statistics with a seven-day chart and a twelve-month heatmap
- Private activity timeline of apps and websites, with a focus-only filter
- Global hotkeys, floating display, launch at login
- English and German interface
